## 10. Shuffled-label control (audit L13)

Models trained with the selected settings on randomly permuted training labels. They should learn nothing. **Log-loss is the clean check**: every shuffled model is worse than the Dummy. Their macro-F1 can sit slightly above the Dummy's because the Dummy always predicts check_call (macro-F1 = check_call F1 / 5), while any model that sometimes guesses other *legal* actions earns some minority-class F1 by chance. The 'random legal guess' reference (actions drawn at random from the training prior, restricted to legal actions) and the 'uniform' version (every legal action equally likely, which class-balanced weighting tends toward) show the size of that effect.
